Privacy Policy

Jake's Boarding School Rules
Effective Date: May 12, 2026  ·  Last Updated: May 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS application Jake's Boarding School Rules (the “App”) handles information. The App is developed and published by NovaVista (the “Developer”).

1. The short version

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. It does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies. Everything you do in the App stays on your device.

2. What data the App stores on your device

The App stores the following data locally on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults system. None of this data leaves your device:

  • Game progress — the set of death endings and main endings you have unlocked.
  • Language preference — whether you have chosen Chinese or English.
  • Purchase entitlement (cached) — a local boolean indicating that you have purchased the “Full Game Unlock” in-app purchase. The authoritative record of this purchase is maintained by Apple via your Apple ID.

You can erase all of this at any time by deleting and reinstalling the App.

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers one non-consumable in-app purchase: Full Game Unlock (Product ID: NovaVista.JakeSchoolHorror.fullgame), which unlocks Level 5 through the final endings.

All payment processing, transaction records, and refund handling are performed by Apple through StoreKit. The Developer does not receive or store your payment information, Apple ID, or any personally identifying data from these transactions. For details on how Apple handles these purchases, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Information the App does NOT collect

The App does not collect, request, or transmit any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Your physical or precise location
  • Your device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, etc.)
  • Photos, microphone audio, camera footage, or contacts
  • Crash reports or usage analytics that identify you
  • Any data shared with third parties for advertising or marketing

5. Children's privacy

The App carries a 17+ age rating due to horror themes and depictions of death. It is not directed to children under 13. Because the App does not collect any personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

6. Network access

The App connects to Apple's servers solely to verify in-app purchase status via StoreKit. It does not connect to any servers operated by the Developer or any third party.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, the new version will be published at this URL with an updated “Last Updated” date.

隐私政策

Jake校园规则怪谈
生效日期：2026 年 5 月 12 日  ·  最后更新：2026 年 5 月 12 日

本《隐私政策》说明 iOS 应用 Jake校园规则怪谈（以下简称“本应用”） 如何处理信息。本应用由 NovaVista（以下简称“开发者”）开发与发行。

一、简版

本应用不收集、不传输、不分享任何个人信息。 本应用不使用任何第三方分析 SDK、广告 SDK 或跟踪技术。 您在本应用内的全部操作都仅保留在您的设备上。

二、本应用在您设备上保存的数据

本应用通过 Apple 标准的 UserDefaults 机制在您的本机设备上存储以下信息， 这些数据不会离开您的设备：

  • 游戏进度——您已解锁的死亡结局与主结局列表。
  • 语言偏好——您选择的中文或英文。
  • 购买权益（本地缓存）——一个布尔值，用于记录您是否购买了 “完整版解锁”内购项目。权益的最终权威记录由 Apple 通过您的 Apple ID 维护。

您可以随时通过卸载并重新安装本应用清除上述所有本地数据。

三、应用内购买

本应用提供一项非消耗型内购商品：完整版解锁 （商品 ID：NovaVista.JakeSchoolHorror.fullgame），用于解锁 第 5 关至全部结局。

所有支付处理、交易记录与退款处理均由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 完成。 开发者不会接收或保存您的支付信息、Apple ID 或任何能够识别您身份的个人数据。 关于 Apple 如何处理这些购买，请参阅 Apple 隐私政策。

四、本应用不收集的信息

本应用不会收集、请求或传输以下任何信息：

  • 姓名、邮箱、电话或任何联系方式
  • 物理位置或精确定位信息
  • 设备标识符（IDFA、IDFV 等）
  • 照片、麦克风音频、相机画面或通讯录
  • 含个人身份的崩溃报告或使用统计
  • 任何与第三方共享用于广告或营销的数据

五、儿童隐私

因含恐怖主题与死亡描写，本应用分级为 17+。本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。 由于本应用不收集任何用户的个人信息，因此也不会有意收集任何儿童的个人信息。

六、网络访问

本应用仅会与 Apple 服务器通信，用于通过 StoreKit 校验内购状态。 本应用不会连接到任何由开发者或第三方运营的服务器。

七、政策变更

若本政策发生实质性变更，新版本将在此 URL 处发布， 并同步更新顶部“最后更新”日期。
